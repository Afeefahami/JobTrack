Why JobTrack?

A job search means dozens of applications, each at a different stage. Tabs, spreadsheets and inbox searches only work until the tenth one.

Without a tracker

  • Which day did I apply to that Python role?
  • The job post was taken down and the description is gone.
  • The follow-up email that never got sent.
  • No idea how many interviews turned into offers.

With JobTrack

  • Every application, dated and in one list.
  • The full job description saved with it.
  • Reminders for follow-ups, interviews and deadlines.
  • Charts built from your real results.
